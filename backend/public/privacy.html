Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy describes how Buklin, the equipment and transport work-request app (Android package com.torikdammam.buklin), handles personal information.

Who operates Buklin and how to contact us

Privacy contact: Munny Akther Riya.
For privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests, email riyab1396f@gmail.com.

Information we collect and why

  • Account information: name, phone number, username, email when provided, account role, assigned equipment, and password hashes. Administrators create accounts. We use this information to sign you in, manage access and arrange work.
  • Work and account records: requests, work descriptions, equipment and loading choices, offer amounts, status changes, store numbers, site addresses, start codes, balances, charges, payments recorded by administrators and activity records. These support matching, service delivery, account administration and resolving disputes.
  • Location: saved or selected work-site coordinates and, with device permission, precise GPS location. We use these to locate a work site, show the assigned operator's position to the customer and check arrival before work starts or finishes.
  • Device and connection information: network requests expose your IP address and basic client information to our hosting and map providers. Authentication tokens and language preferences are stored on your device to support sign-in and your chosen language.

Location sharing and permissions

Operators share their location while the active job-location screen is running. On Android, a visible foreground-service notification can keep this sharing active when you switch apps or turn off the screen. Sharing stops when the job screen closes or the job completes. The latest position can remain stored after live sharing stops. You can revoke location permission in Android settings; features requiring location will then be unavailable.

Customers can select a site on a map or use device location to choose it. Account site information may also be entered by an administrator.

Who receives information

  • Work participants: eligible operators see available work details. Exact site details and store numbers are provided to the assigned operator after acceptance. Customers can see their assigned operator's shared location.
  • Buklin administrators: authorized administrators manage accounts, work, balances and service records.
  • Hosting and storage: Render hosts the service and Neon provides the PostgreSQL database used to process and store app information.
  • Maps: OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles. Loading a map sends the map area requested, your IP address and client request information to the tile provider.
  • Notifications, when enabled in a configured app version: OneSignal and Google's Android push infrastructure process push identifiers, a Buklin account identifier, device information and notification content to deliver work alerts. Version 1.0.16 does not configure OneSignal push delivery. Notification permissions can be changed in device settings.

These services may process information in countries other than your own. This app does not include advertising or sell personal information through its app functionality.

Storage, security and retention

The hosted app connects to the service using HTTPS. Passwords are stored as hashes, and authenticated access checks restrict account and work information. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee complete security.

Account information is kept while the account remains active or until an administrator processes deletion. Completed and cancelled work disappears from customer and operator history after 72 hours; this is a display limit, not automatic deletion from the database. Work records, site information, latest job locations, billing records and activity logs can remain stored for service administration and dispute handling. The current service does not apply a fixed automatic deletion period to these records.

Request account or data deletion

Email riyab1396f@gmail.com with the subject “Buklin account deletion” and your username or registered phone number. You can also request access to or correction of your information using this address. Do not send your password or work start codes. We may need to verify account ownership.

Account deletion is handled by an administrator and removes the account profile, sign-in sessions and account-linked balance entries. It cancels active work and removes account links from work and activity records. Shared work records, site details and last job locations are not automatically erased by that action. Include any request to remove this associated information in your message so it can be reviewed, including any records needed for another participant, an unresolved dispute or a legal obligation. Uninstalling the app does not delete information held by the service.
